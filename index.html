Some colors only show up after time has washed the rest away.

Sunbleached is a film-look preset app for iPhone. Pick a preset, drag the intensity, save to Photos. That's the whole flow.

No subscription. No ads. No watermark.

What it does

  • 43 film presets — grouped into three families: Light, Faded and Grain. You're not scrolling through hundreds.
  • Intensity slider — every preset goes from 0 to 100. Nothing is all-or-nothing.
  • Full-resolution export — no watermark, no downscaling. The free tier exports at full quality too.
  • Three languages — English, Simplified Chinese and Traditional Chinese, switchable inside the app.
  • Works offline — airplane mode included. Nothing is uploaded, so nothing needs a connection.

What it doesn't do

  • No ads — no splash screen, no banners, no pop-ups.
  • No subscription — you pay once, it's yours. No monthly charge.
  • No virtual currency — no gems, no credits, no second payment.
  • No uploading your photos — everything is processed on your iPhone.
  • No account — open it and start.

Three promises

Buy once, yours forever. One-time purchase, permanent access, including presets added later.
Free presets never become paid. Nothing in the free tier will be locked away in a future update.
Works with no connection. No sign-in, no network, no problem.
