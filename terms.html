1. What the app does

Sunbleached is a photo editor that runs entirely on your device. It applies film-look presets to your photos and lets you blend them. It provides:

  • Preset application with an intensity slider;
  • Preset recipes built from exposure, contrast, saturation, temperature, vignette and grain, blended as a whole by the intensity slider;
  • Full-resolution export, saved locally.

It does not provide: cloud storage or cross-device sync, an account system, a social community, a full RAW colour pipeline, or professional-grade selections and masking. If you need those, use a professional desktop tool.
