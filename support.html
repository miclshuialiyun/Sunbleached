Support

Email us directly: miclshui@gmail.com
We usually reply within two business days. Including your device model, iOS version and app version makes it much faster to track down.

System requirements

  • iOS 17.0 or later
  • iPhone
  • Fully offline — no connection needed

Frequently asked questions

I switched phones or reinstalled — do I have to pay again?

No. The purchase is tied to your Apple ID. Open the app and go to Settings → Restore Purchases. No extra charge.

How do I restore my purchase?

Two places: the "Restore Purchases" button on the paywall, and Settings → Restore Purchases. You need a connection once for verification; after that the app is offline again. If it fails, check that the Apple ID you're signed in with is the one you purchased with.

Will I be charged monthly?

No. Sunbleached is a one-time purchase. There is no subscription and no auto-renewal. You pay once.

Is the free version usable, or will it get locked later?

It's usable. The free tier includes 12 presets, the intensity slider and full-resolution export, free forever. Our promise: nothing in the free tier will be moved behind the paywall in a later update.

Does export add a watermark or compress the image?

No. Both the free and full versions export at full resolution with no watermark.

Does the app need access to my whole photo library?

No. Choosing a photo goes through Apple's system photo picker, so the app only ever receives the single image you hand it. It also cannot read your library in the background.

Are my photos uploaded anywhere?

No. Everything is processed on your iPhone. There is no server receiving your photos. See the Privacy Policy.

Are there ads?

None. No splash ads, no banners, no pop-up promotions.

Do I need an account?

No. Open and use. We don't collect an email, phone number or any identity information.

Can I change the app language?

Yes. Settings → Language gives you Follow System, English, 简体中文 and 繁體中文. The change applies immediately, no restart. Preset names stay in English on purpose — they're proper names, not descriptions.

Does it support RAW?

The first version targets everyday JPEG and HEIC photos, not a full RAW pipeline. We're not promising RAW as a future addition.

Will the preset list grow until I can't find anything?

Presets are grouped into three families — Light, Faded and Grain. We're not building an endlessly expanding preset store.

Can I get a refund?

All payments are handled by Apple and we cannot issue refunds. Please request one through reportaproblem.apple.com. We can help you prepare the materials.

The app crashes or shows a blank screen

Update to the latest version first. If it persists, email miclshui@gmail.com with your device model, iOS version, app version and the steps to reproduce. A screenshot helps.

I want to suggest a feature or report a bug

Please do — email miclshui@gmail.com. It's a one-person project, so replies may not be instant, but every message gets read.

Data and privacy

We collect no data. Your photos never leave your device. Full details in the Privacy Policy; licensing and refund terms in the Terms of Use.

Purchasing and restoring

  • Purchase: completed on the in-app paywall, paid through Apple.
  • Restore: "Restore Purchases" in Settings or at the bottom of the paywall. Free and repeatable.
  • Cancel a subscription: not applicable — this app has no subscription.
  • Refund: only through Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
