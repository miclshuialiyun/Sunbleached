Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-10-17　·　Last updated: 2026-10-17

There is no data for us to collect. Your photos are processed on your device only. They are never uploaded, and we have no server that could receive them.

2. Permissions, one by one

PermissionWhen it is requestedWhat it is forWhere data goes
Photo library — addingThe first time you tap "Save to Photos"Writing the edited result into your libraryStays on device
Photo library — readingWhen you tap "Choose Photo"Loading the one image you pickedStays on device

Selecting a photo goes through Apple's out-of-process photo picker. The app never gets access to your whole library — only the single image you hand it. Sunbleached does not request camera, location, contacts, microphone or notification access.

All permissions are requested only when you trigger the matching action, never at launch. You can revoke them any time in iOS Settings → Sunbleached; the rest of the app keeps working.

3. What we don't do

  • We don't collect or upload your photos or editing content.
  • We don't include any third-party analytics, crash-reporting or advertising SDK.
  • We don't collect IDFA, IDFV or any device identifier.
  • We don't read contacts, location, calendar, reminders or health data.
  • We don't require an account, and we don't collect your name, email or phone number.
  • We don't use your photos to train any model.
  • We don't sell or share data with anyone — there is no data to share.

4. In-app purchase

Sunbleached offers a one-time, non-consumable purchase. Payment, billing and refunds are all handled by Apple. We never see your payment details, and we cannot issue refunds.

To request a refund, submit it through Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com. We're happy to help you prepare the request, but the decision is Apple's.

5. Data retention and deletion

  • Your photos stay on your device. We hold no copy.
  • Deleting the app removes its local preferences and caches. Photos you already saved to your library are unaffected.
  • Because we hold nothing, there is nothing for us to delete on request — the act of deleting the app is the deletion.

6. Children's privacy

Sunbleached is intended for a general audience. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Since we collect no data at all, no children's data can be collected.

7. Your rights

Because we hold no data about you, the rights to access, correct, delete and port data under GDPR / CCPA are satisfied by design — there is nothing to access or delete. If you are in a relevant jurisdiction and still have questions, email us at the address below.

8. Changes to this policy

If future features change how data is handled, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above. Material changes will also be surfaced inside the app.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy: miclshui@gmail.com. We usually reply within two business days.
